Statutory rates and limits (editable)

Defaults are the rates verified on 17 Nov 2026. If the law changes, change the value here and run again.

PF ECR is matched on UAN and the ESIC file on the ESIC No (IP number). Bank statements are matched on name only (spaces and Mr./Mrs. ignored). The wage sheet Net and the Statement Net are shown side by side, so you can compare them yourself. The statement amount is taken from the matched line, treating the last number on a line as the balance. The matched bank line is shown under each result so you can verify it. If the statement file has a FILE_PAY_AMT column, that amount is used as the Statement Net, and Responsibility For Payment Of Wages compares only the wage sheet Net with it. Maharashtra compliance checks: PF is checked on the earned (actual) values, never the fixed salary columns: PF wages = Total Gross − HRA − Attendance Bonus − Statutory Bonus − Total OT, at 12%, up to the PF ceiling (₹25,000 from 17 Sep 2026, ₹15,000 before). ESIC is 0.75% up to ₹21,000 gross. PT is read from the PT column using the Maharashtra slabs (₹300 in February). LWF is ₹25 in June and December. Minimum wage is checked on the fixed basic wage (plus fixed DA if it has its own column), which is the monthly wage rate, against the slab you enter. Earned basic is not used for this check, so part-month employees are not flagged. If the sheet has no fixed basic column, earned basic is used. OT limit: 38 hours a month (115 per quarter).

How it works
